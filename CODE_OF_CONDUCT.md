# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — is expected to
behave professionally and in good faith.

That means: be direct about code and generous about people. Criticise the work, never the person.
Assume the other reader is competent and reading carefully. Say what you actually mean, and say
when you do not know.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity — including age, disability,
  ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions without new evidence, or
  demanding unpaid attention from maintainers.

## Scope

Project spaces — this repository and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the address on the organisation's GitHub profile, with `conduct` in the subject. Reports
are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may warn, temporarily
block, or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement is proportionate and explained to the person
it applies to.
