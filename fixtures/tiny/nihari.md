# Nihari

Nihari is a slow-cooked stew of beef or lamb shank, traditionally simmered overnight with bone
marrow and a spice blend led by ginger, fennel and long pepper. It is served at breakfast,
garnished with julienned ginger, green chilli and lemon.

## Origin

Nihari is associated with Old Delhi and Lucknow, and after 1947 became a signature of Karachi,
where it is eaten as a weekend breakfast.

## Preparation

The shank is cooked on low heat for six to eight hours. The gravy is thickened with a roasted
wheat-flour slurry called *aata*.
