# Halwa Puri

Halwa puri is not one dish but a breakfast set: puffed fried *puri* bread, sweet semolina *halwa*,
and a lightly spiced chickpea curry (*cholay*), often with a potato bhujia.

## The ritual

It is a weekend and holiday breakfast, bought from a *halwai* in the morning rush rather than made
at home. The sweet-and-savoury combination on one plate is the point.
