# Chapli Kabab

Chapli kabab is a flat, wide minced-beef patty from Peshawar, shallow-fried in its own fat until
the edges crisp. Crushed coriander seed, dried pomegranate and slices of tomato are pressed into
the surface.

## Origin

The name likely derives from *chaprikh*, Pashto for flat. Landi Kotal and Namak Mandi in Peshawar
are the reference points.

## Serving

Served with naan, sliced onion and green chutney.
