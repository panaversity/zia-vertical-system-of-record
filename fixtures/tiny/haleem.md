# Haleem

Haleem is a thick porridge of wheat, barley, four lentils and meat, cooked for hours and then
pounded smooth. It is garnished with fried onion, ginger, coriander and lemon.

## Occasions

Haleem is prepared in bulk for Muharram and Ramadan, and sold year-round in Karachi and Lahore.

## Texture

The defining test is the *resha* — the meat must pull into silky threads through the grain base.
