# Karahi

Karahi takes its name from the heavy wok-like pan it is cooked in. Goat or chicken is cooked fast
over high heat with tomatoes, ginger and green chilli — no onions in the classic Peshawari style.

## The debate

Whether onions belong in karahi divides kitchens; the Peshawari orthodoxy says tomatoes, ginger,
green chilli and nothing else.

## Serving

Eaten straight from the karahi with naan, never rice.
