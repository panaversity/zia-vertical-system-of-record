# Samosa

The samosa is a fried triangular pastry, filled with spiced potato and peas or with minced meat.
It arrived in South Asia from Central Asia by the thirteenth century.

## At the table

Eaten as a street snack and at iftar, with tamarind and mint chutneys. In Karachi, *samosa chaat*
submerges it in chickpeas, yoghurt and chutney.
