# Paya

Paya is a curry of goat or beef trotters simmered overnight until the collagen renders the gravy
sticky and rich. It is a winter breakfast dish, eaten with naan.

## Preparation

The trotters are flame-cleaned, then simmered eight to twelve hours with onion, ginger-garlic and
whole spices. The gravy's body comes entirely from the bones.
