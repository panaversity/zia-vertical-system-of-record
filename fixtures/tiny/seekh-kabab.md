# Seekh Kabab

Seekh kabab is spiced minced meat — beef or lamb — pressed by hand along flat iron skewers and
grilled over open coals. The fat content is the craft: too lean and it crumbles, too fat and it
flares.

## Serving

Slid off the skewer onto naan with sliced onion, mint chutney and a squeeze of lemon.
