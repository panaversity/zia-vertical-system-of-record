# Lassi

Lassi is a churned yoghurt drink of rural Punjab, served salted (*namkeen*) or sweet, in tall
metal glasses, often topped with a spoon of cream (*malai*) or a knob of butter.

## Variants

The salted version cuts rich food in summer; the sweet version is closer to a meal. Mango lassi
is a restaurant invention that travelled.
