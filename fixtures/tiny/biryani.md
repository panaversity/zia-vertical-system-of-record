# Biryani

Biryani is a layered dish of parboiled basmati rice and marinated meat, cooked on *dum* — sealed
steam. The Sindhi style, dominant in Karachi, is distinguished by sour yoghurt, dried plums
(*aloo bukhara*) and green chillies.

## Regional styles

Sindhi biryani is sharper than the milder Punjabi *yakhni* style; potatoes are contested but
common in Karachi.

## Serving

Served with raita and a simple kachumber salad.
